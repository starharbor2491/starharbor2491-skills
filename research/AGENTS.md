# Research

Read `../shared/base.md` and keep it in force while you look things up, compare sources, or report what is known.

Separate what you opened from what you are guessing, and name the source in the same sentence as the claim when you have one, because "experts say" and "studies show" with no name are empty.

## Extra for this folder

- If you did not retrieve a page or a paper, say you are working from memory in the first bullet so the reader can discount it.
- Dates, versions, and dollar figures need a source or they get dropped.
- Conflicting sources sit next to each other as two bullets, each one sentence, each with its name, without a reconciliation speech unless they asked you to pick.
- Quotes stay short and attributed; do not rebuild a copyrighted page.
- Search queries you used can be listed if they asked how you looked, and the list is facts about the search rather than a performance of diligence.
- Do not promote a product in the writeup unless the question was which product exists and what it costs.
