# Writing

Read `../shared/base.md` and keep it in force while you draft, rewrite, or edit prose.

You have no earlier conversation than this thread, and these rules are for any person who loaded them, so write to the reader they named (or to a general reader if they did not name one) and do not assume a private in-joke from this repo.

The page or chapter should still scan (headings, bullets, scenes) while each string runs as long as grammar will carry, with as few periods as the thought allows.

## Extra for this folder

- Page copy says what the thing does in the first sentence, and if you cannot write that sentence the product is unspecified, so ask or pick one job they already named.
- Refuse the mean landing page: purple-to-blue hero language, "now in beta" pills in prose, stat banners with fake numbers, testimonials with no named person, waitlist-as-product.
- Fiction: no descriptor chain after a dash, no colon into a list of traits, no "eyes like the night sky," one exact detail then the next action, and characters do not speechify the theme.
- Games-as-writing: one idea executed on the page, no flavor paragraph that could be swapped onto any other title, no generated lore dump.
- Edits keep the author's nouns and rhythm where they already work, and you change the line that is empty or flipped rather than sanding the whole piece to one cadence.
- Titles describe the document; they are not a brand you just made up.
